Travel is better

This website was created as part of a web development learning journey, exploring the craft of building a thoughtful, multi-page travel experience with HTML5 and CSS3.

Every page brings together the details that make a trip feel considered: clear planning, inspiring destinations, useful media and a consistent visual language.

FayTech Travels is a practice project with a real point of view: good travel should feel exciting before you even leave home.
